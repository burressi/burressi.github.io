The Challenge

In a long endurance race, results are often decided in pit lane: when to stop, how much fuel to add, when tires are falling off, and how to react to cautions. With 30+ cars and multiple classes on track, the team needed a reliable way to turn lap-by-lap data into decisions in real time.

My Role

  • Lead race strategy for the car, including fuel planning, stint length, and pit windows.
  • Track lap times and race pace, and communicate with mechanics during pit stops to guide adjustments.
  • Manage overall timing so the crew, drivers, and car stay aligned for pre-grid procedures and post-race inspections.

The Strategy Calculator

I designed a strategy spreadsheet that tracks manually entered data for two different vehicle classes. It models race pace, lap times, tire degradation, and fuel consumption, which lets the team project pit windows and compare our pace against the competition as the race develops. A running track sheet carries lessons forward from round to round across the season.
